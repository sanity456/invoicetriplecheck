Title
ThreeWayLineMatch - Role-Separated PO Receipt Invoice Reconciliation

Notes / Description
Built ThreeWayLineMatch, a reusable intelligent contract. Its GenLayer-native mechanism is buyer PO -> receiver attestation + supplier invoice -> dual crosswalk consensus -> deterministic discrepancy codes. Correction: The validator now recomputes both crosswalks and their digest from the attached mappings, independently compares the full payload, and revalidates it before reconciliation. Inputs and source references are public, caller-supplied, and unverified. It does not approve payment, detect fraud, validate tax, establish delivery, or replace accounting controls. GenVM lint and strict typecheck pass; 11 direct tests and 1 five-validator integration test pass. StudioNet completed 6 successful isolated-wallet transactions. Its source and full ABI/schema match the pinned evidence exactly. The earlier deployment is superseded.
